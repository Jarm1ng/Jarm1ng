# Hi, I'm Jiaming Feng

I work on medical image analysis, surgical computer vision, and geometry-aware AI systems for image-guided intervention. My current research focuses on preoperative-to-intraoperative registration, 3D anatomical landmark segmentation, and augmented or mixed-reality guidance for liver surgery.

I am currently an MSc Advanced Computer Science student at the University of Leeds and a research collaborator with the AI in Medicine and Surgery Group.

## Research Focus

- Medical image analysis and surgical scene understanding
- Preoperative-to-intraoperative image fusion
- 3D anatomical landmark segmentation
- 3D-2D registration for laparoscopic surgery
- Geometry-aware deep learning on meshes and point clouds
- Augmented and mixed reality for surgical guidance

## Selected Research

**GeoTranMesh**
Geometry-guided multi-branch mesh Transformer for 3D liver surface landmark segmentation. The method integrates hierarchical mesh encoding, directional feature fusion, and multi-task supervision for anatomy-aware segmentation on liver mesh data.

**Vis2Reg**
Visibility-aware, landmark-free geometric 3D-2D registration framework for laparoscopic liver surgery. The project uses differentiable rasterization and mask-guided self-supervision to improve alignment under severe partial visibility.

**Dynamic liver registration**
Ongoing work on robust video-based preoperative-to-intraoperative liver registration for dynamic surgical scenes, extending static geometric fusion towards temporally consistent intraoperative guidance.
